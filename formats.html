What comes out.

One MOV per matched video. Separate tracks for separate recordings. Originals untouched.

Each track keeps its sample rate and channel count. Resampling changes samples; integer output is dithered when needed. If integer output could clip, that track uses 32-bit float instead, with a warning. Float headroom is retained. No normalization or downmixing.

32-bit integer, 64-bit float, unidentified lossless precision and multi-track standalone recordings are not supported. 32-bit integer is different from 32-bit float.

Video & metadata

The first video track is copied without re-encoding. If it cannot be written to MOV, export fails visibly. Camera audio is replaced; extra video, subtitle and timecode tracks are omitted.

Orientation and some metadata are copied, but complete metadata, camera-specific log/HDR information and Dolby Vision preservation are not guaranteed. Keep your originals for grading and finishing.

File size & matching

Audio output is uncompressed, so compressed recordings can grow substantially. Short recordings store only their overlap, positioned on the video timeline. Temporary processing files also need free disk space.

One recording can match several videos, or several recordings can match one video. Overlaps remain separate tracks. Duplicates can produce duplicate results. Players and editors may select or mix tracks differently.

Matching needs clear shared sound. Silence, repeated sound, edits and changing drift can prevent a reliable match. Short overlaps may align without drift correction.

Unmatched videos and wholly unused recordings are copied unchanged. Unused portions of a matched recording are not exported separately. The report lists matches, output formats, conversions, omissions and errors.

Supported & tested

Video containers: MOV, MP4, M4V.
Recordings: WAV, AIFF, CAF, FLAC, M4A, AAC, MP3.
The codec and profile must be supported by macOS.

Tested fixtures include H.264, HEVC and ProRes 422 HQ; PCM16/24, float32, FLAC, ALAC, AAC and MP3; 44.1–192 kHz; mono, stereo and eight channels. Checks cover drift, channel separation, mixed-track output, timing, rotation and unchanged encoded video.

These are specific test cases, not every codec profile or editor. Raw AAC, M4V, unusual channel layouts and complete HDR workflows are not fully qualified. Camera RAW, MKV and WebM are outside the supported list.
