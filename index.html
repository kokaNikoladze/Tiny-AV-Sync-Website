Synchronize a folder

Drop a folder into Tiny AV Sync or choose it with Choose Folder. Include camera clips with audible reference sound and the separate recordings from the same takes. Matching needs clear, overlapping sound.

Tiny AV Sync compares the recordings, estimates their time offset and corrects reliable, steady clock drift by resampling. Short overlaps may be aligned without drift correction. Media processing is local; no media upload or separate app account is needed. Purchases use your Apple Account.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 15 or later.

Trial and lifetime unlock

Formats & limitations

What happens to your files — output formats, file sizes, metadata, tested inputs and limitations.

Video containers: MOV, MP4, M4V. Recordings: WAV, AIFF, CAF, FLAC, M4A, AAC, MP3. The codec and profile inside the file must also be supported by macOS.

Build 10 exports all matched recordings as 32-bit float PCM and pads each track to the video’s duration. A revision with per-recording PCM formats and compact audio tracks has passed its automated checks; it is not yet available in TestFlight.

Results

Results go into a new synced folder. Existing results and originals are not overwritten. Unmatched videos and unused audio are kept separately. avsync-report.json records matches, timing corrections, skipped files and warnings.

One recording may match several videos, and a video may have several matching recordings. Overlapping recordings remain separate tracks. Duplicate files can produce duplicate outputs or tracks. Listen to the results before editing.

Silence, unrelated or repeated sound, very short overlaps, edits and changing clock speeds may not produce a reliable match. Check Details for explanations. Cancel stops the current job; completed outputs remain.

Help

If folder access fails, choose the folder again. If there is not enough storage, free space for temporary audio and the new output files. Keep your originals.

Email contact@koka.one with your Mac model, macOS version, Tiny AV Sync version, the exact message and the steps that led to it. Include the report if useful; it contains file names. Send media only if needed and you choose to share it.
